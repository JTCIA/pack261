## Pack Meeting Schedule

Pack meetings are held the **first Tuesday** of each month at 7:00 PM.
Location: St. Thomas Community Center, 123 Main St.

### Pack Meeting Agenda Template

1. **Opening** (5 min) — Pledge of Allegiance, Scout Oath/Law
2. **Cubmaster Welcome** (5 min) — Announcements, recognitions
3. **Den Presentations** (20 min) — Rotating dens share what they've learned
4. **Main Activity** (15 min) — Group activity tied to monthly theme
5. **Advancement Ceremony** (10 min) — Rank advancements and awards
6. **Closing** (5 min) — Cubmaster minute, Scout sign, adjourn
