# Getting Started as a Leader

Welcome to the Pack 261 leadership team! This guide will help you get oriented and ready to lead.

## Step 1: Complete Your Training

All registered leaders must complete:

- **Youth Protection Training (YPT)** — Required within 30 days of registration. Complete at [my.scouting.org](https://my.scouting.org)
- **Position-Specific Training** — Den Leader Essentials or Pack Committee training depending on your role
- **Hazardous Weather Training** — Required for camping activities

## Step 2: Register on my.Scouting.org

- Create or update your account at [my.scouting.org](https://my.scouting.org)
- Accept your leadership position invitation from the Cubmaster
- Set up your training profile

## Step 3: Join Communication Channels

- **Pack GroupMe/Email List** — Ask the Cubmaster to add you
- **Den Leader Chat** — Coordination between dens
- **Google Drive** — Shared resources folder (link in Resources section)

## Step 4: Review Key Documents

- Pack Charter and bylaws
- Annual program plan
- Budget overview
- BSA Guide to Safe Scouting

## Step 5: Meet Your Den

As a Den Leader:
- Review your scouts' advancement records in Scoutbook
- Schedule your first den meeting (coordinate with the pack calendar)
- Connect with families to introduce yourself
