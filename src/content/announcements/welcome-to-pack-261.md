Welcome to Cub Scout Pack 261! We're thrilled to kick off another fantastic scouting year. Whether you're returning scouts or joining us for the first time, we have an incredible year of adventures planned.

## What to Expect This Year

- **Monthly Pack Meetings** — Usually the Third Wednesday of every month at 7:00 PM
- **Den Meetings** — Monthly, schedule varies by den (check with your Den Leader)
- **Two Campouts** — Fall and Spring
- **Pinewood Derby** 
- **Blue & Gold Banquet** — February, celebrating scouting and advancements

## Getting Started

If you're new to the pack, please make sure you've:
1. Registered on [my.scouting.org](https://my.scouting.org)
2. Paid your pack dues
3. Connected with your Den Leader
4. Joined our pack GroupMe

We look forward to a year full of learning, service, and adventure. **Yours in Scouting!**
