Calling all scouts — it's popcorn time! Our annual Trail's End Popcorn fundraiser is our biggest fundraiser of the year, and every scout is encouraged to participate.

## Why Sell Popcorn?

- **50% of proceeds** stay with our pack to fund activities, awards, and campouts
- Scouts who sell **$300+** earn their camp fees paid for next summer
- Top sellers earn prizes including gift cards, electronics, and a trip to Scout camp

## How It Works

1. Pick up your order form at the next pack or den meeting
2. Take orders from neighbors, family, and coworkers
3. All orders due by **October 25th**
4. Product delivery estimated for **November 10th**

## Online Selling

Don't want to knock on doors? Scouts can sell online! Visit [trails-end.com](https://www.trails-end.com) and set up your online storefront — friends and family can order from anywhere in the country.

Let's have our best year yet! 🍿
